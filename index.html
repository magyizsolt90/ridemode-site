Privacy Policy for RideMode

Last updated: July 2026

RideMode is a motorcycle dashboard app. This policy explains what data the app accesses, why, and what happens to it.

What we collect and why

Precise location. Used to show your speed, heading, and route, and to log your rides locally. Also used to fetch the current weather and, occasionally, to look up the name of the street you're on.

Background location, while a ride is active. If you switch to a navigation app mid-ride (for example, because you got lost), RideMode keeps recording your route in the background instead of leaving a gap. This only happens during an active ride — it stops as soon as the ride ends, and it's never used to track you outside of a ride. This requires the "Always" location permission; you can decline it and RideMode still works, it just won't keep recording route data while another app is in front.

Motion & fitness data. Used to calculate lean angle, detect corners, and detect when you've started or stopped riding. Not used for step counting or any other purpose.

Where your data goes

Nowhere, by default. Your ride history, settings, and app logs are stored only on your device, and are never uploaded to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes this data permanently. You can also delete individual rides from within the app at any time.

The only data that ever leaves your device does so to Apple's own first-party services, to make specific features work:

— Your location is sent to Apple WeatherKit to fetch current weather.
— Your location is occasionally sent to Apple's geocoding service to look up a street name.

These calls go directly to Apple, governed by Apple's own privacy terms — we never see this data ourselves.

If you use the in-app feedback form, your message is currently stored only in the app's local session log on your device. It is not transmitted anywhere. If this changes in a future update — for example, to let us actually receive your feedback — this policy will be updated first, and you'll be able to review it before that update reaches you.

No accounts, no tracking, no ads

RideMode has no login, no user accounts, and no advertising or analytics SDKs. We don't track you across apps or websites, and we have no way to identify who you are.

Children's privacy

RideMode is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If what RideMode collects changes in a future update, this policy will be updated to match before that update ships, not after.
